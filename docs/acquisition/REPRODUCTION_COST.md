## Why acquisition beats greenfield

Reproducing `patentpulse` means rediscovering domain constraints, re-implementing architecture and edge cases, rebuilding tests/CI/packaging, and rewriting diligence materials already present in this repository. Buying the transferable IP is the faster path to a production-ready starting point.

## Product thesis

PatentPulse is a locally owned, 1.6 TB corpus of USPTO patent grants and published applications. It downloads official weekly XML dumps, stream-parses them without loading an archive into memory, and writes normalized SQLite and JSON Lines outputs for search, tokenization, embeddings, and model training.
